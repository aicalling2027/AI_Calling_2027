1. PRODUCT OVERVIEW 
1.1 Introduction 
OWN AI is a multilingual AI Calling SaaS platform designed to help businesses automate inbound and 
outbound voice conversations through intelligent AI employees. 
The platform enables organizations to create, configure, train, deploy, and manage AI voice employees 
capable of performing business operations such as: 
• Lead Generation 
• Customer Support 

=== PAGE BREAK ===

• Sales Calling 
• Appointment Scheduling 
• Follow-up Calls 
• Customer Qualification 
• Information Collection 
• Business Communication 
• Inbound Call Handling 
• Outbound Call Campaigns 
Users can create AI employees using custom prompts, select supported languages, configure voices, 
select AI models, provide knowledge sources, and connect phone numbers. 
The platform will provide a centralized dashboard to monitor calls, leads, conversations, employee 
performance, usage, and billing. 
1.2 Product Vision 
To build a scalable multilingual AI Calling platform that enables businesses across India and other 
markets to automate voice-based customer interactions using configurable AI employees. 
1.3 Product Goals 
1. Build a complete AI Employee creation and management system. 
2. Support multilingual AI voice conversations, targeting 15+ Indian languages. 
3. Enable inbound and outbound AI calling. 
4. Provide instant lead calling and bulk campaign management. 
5. Allow users to train AI employees with business-specific knowledge. 
6. Provide call analytics, transcripts, recordings, and summaries. 
7. Support CRM and third-party integrations. 
8. Automate business workflows using n8n. 
9. Provide organization-level management and multi-tenant security. 
10. Implement subscription and usage-based billing. 
1.4 Product Design Principles 
• Simple and intuitive user experience. 
• Modular and maintainable architecture. 
• Secure multi-tenant design. 

=== PAGE BREAK ===

• Provider-independent AI integration. 
• Scalable calling infrastructure. 
• Reliable background processing. 
• API-first backend. 
• Clear usage and billing transparency.

OWN AI will use a Monorepo architecture with a Modular Monolith backend initially. 
The repository will contain: 
• Frontend. 
• Backend. 
• AI Engine. 
• Background Workers. 
• Automation.
